As you start exploring and participating, we just ask that you keep a few things in mind: please respect the site and the people who use it. We aim to keep this a helpful, welcoming, and constructive environment for fly fishers of all skill levels.

If you would like to contribute to the site with things like logs, hatch sightings and pictures, please reach out to admin@StillwaterFlyFishing.com and we can add you to the contributors list.

Please start by:
Step #1 - Go to your profile and set your home address.
    - click on your name in the top right corner and edit your profile
    - Use a small picture as your avatar so we can get to know you. - optional
    - set your home address so the distances, times and local forcasts will be accurate. - optional

Step #2 - Click on "Fishing Logs" in the top left corner to go to the home page.

Step #3 - Add a lake to your favorites.
    - Click on lakes
    - Either search for a lake in the search bar or choose a lake from the "Lakes by district"
    - on the fourth row down, click on the "Add Lake" button.
    - Click on the "back" button at the top left of the page to return to the main lakes list.
    - The lake you just added should now be on your "Favorites" list. Click on it to view its details.

Step #4 - Add a lake to My Region.
    - Click on regions in the top left corner to go to the home page.
    - Click on the "edit" button in the top right.
    - Chan ge the name if you want and chan ge the base address for this region.
    - Add a lake to the region.
 
Step #5 - Click on LOGO in the top left corner to go to the home page.
    
Step #6 - Plan a trip to a lake.
    - click on a lake.
    - Select a week in the future and click on "plan"
    
Step #7 - Wander around and discover all the different parts of the website.

ENJOY!
